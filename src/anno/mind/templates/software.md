# Project

## Aim

What are we building, and why? The problem, and the intent in a sentence or two.
Same shape whether this is one feature or a whole suite.

## Success

What does success look like? The observable outcomes that mean it's done well —
how we'll know it works.

## Focus

What's in, and what's explicitly out. The boundary that keeps it on track.

## Approach

How it comes together — key pieces, data, interfaces, tech choices. Just enough
to start building.

## Risks & Unknowns

What could go wrong, open questions, and decisions still to make.
